# Sibyl — Speak, and be remembered

Imagine the things you say when no one is listening. 

The rambling thoughts at 2 AM, the sudden sparks of inspiration during a walk, the quiet confessions you aren't ready to share, and the memories that feel too fleeting to write down. Most of us let these moments vanish into the air. 

**Sibyl is where those moments live.**

Sibyl isn't just a voice recorder; it is a local-first, open-source voice diary for Android. It was built for the people who think out loud—those who find speaking more natural than typing, and who want a sanctuary for their spoken thoughts.

## The Story of Your Voice

Most apps treat your voice as data to be uploaded, analyzed, and stored on a server halfway across the world. Sibyl treats your voice as a secret.

Everything you say stays on your device. No accounts, no cloud sync, no corporate eyes. Just you and your thoughts, organized exactly how you want them.

### Why Sibyl?

- **A Sanctuary for Thought**: Use custom tags, colors, and naming rules to turn a flat list of recordings into a structured map of your mind.
- **The Power of Memory**: Search through your diary not just by filename, but by the actual words you spoke.
- **Non-Destructive Reflection**: Trim, merge, and append your recordings. Your original thoughts remain untouched, allowing you to edit your narrative without losing the original moment.
- **Your Key, Your Rules**: Want a transcript? Sibyl supports AI transcription using an API key that *you* control. The app only makes a network request when you explicitly ask it to.
- **True Ownership**: With optional folder syncing, your recordings exist as real files on your phone. You own the data; you own the memory.
